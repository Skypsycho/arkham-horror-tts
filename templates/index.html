How this app works

Arkham Horror TTS turns the read-aloud text of Arkham Horror: The Card Game campaign guides into narrated audio. It covers intros, resolutions, interludes, and the flavour text on agenda and act cards. You can play it at the table instead of reading it out yourself.

Getting started

  1. Settings: enter your ElevenLabs API key and a voice ID, and choose a voice model.
  2. Import → Download from FFG: pick a campaign. The app downloads the official guide PDF and pulls out every read-aloud passage, grouped by scenario. You can also upload a PDF yourself.
  3. Import → ArkhamDB cards: select the campaign's packs to add the agenda and act card texts (front and back) to each scenario.
  4. Check the text before generating audio. Generation uses ElevenLabs credits, so make sure the passages read right first.
  5. Generate audio in the Library (Edit mode) with each entry's ⚡ Generate audio button.

Library: Play mode (at the table)

  • Pick a campaign on the left, then a scenario from the list.
  • Intro: the big button at the top plays the scenario's opening text.
  • Agenda and Act cards show the card you're on. Press ▶ to hear the front, Flip to back › when the card advances, then ▶ again and Next card ›. Use ‹ to step back if you went too far.
  • Each scenario remembers where its decks are, even after closing the browser. Reset decks starts them over.
  • Resolutions and other passages are listed below the cards.
  • ‹ Previous / Next › move between scenarios. ‹ Scenarios goes back to the list.
  • ¶ shows the text, so you can read along.

Library: Edit mode (preparing a campaign)

  • Switch with the Play / Edit toggle above the scenarios.
  • Click an entry's text to correct it. It saves when you click away.
  • ✎ renames entries, scenarios and groups. Drag the handles to reorder or move entries between scenarios.
  • ⚡ generates audio for one entry, ↻ regenerates it, and 📎 attaches your own recording instead.
  • Deleting asks first, and keeps the audio files by default.

Good to know

  • The bar at the top right shows your remaining ElevenLabs characters and when they reset.
  • The player at the bottom shows what's playing. Click its progress bar to jump.
  • Only you can log in. Log out is next to the credits bar.
